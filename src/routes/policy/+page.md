# Policy

> **Draft for project partner review.** This page is not a final policy. The project partners should confirm the text and complete the sections below before publication.

## About this resource

The DSA Case Law Tracker is a research resource for private enforcement cases under the EU Digital Services Act. Case summaries and classifications provide research context; they are not legal advice. Consult the original sources when interpreting a decision.

## Editorial review

Case suggestions are submitted for editorial review and are not published automatically. Public entries include source references where available. Records may be updated as the research team reviews information or new material becomes available.

## Sources and reuse

Linked documents and third-party materials remain subject to their original terms and rights. Inclusion in this tracker does not itself grant permission to reuse them.
