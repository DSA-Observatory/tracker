## What the tracker handles

The DSA Case Law Tracker publishes case records assembled from public legal sources and submitted source material. A case suggestion can include a case reference, source links, an explanation of its relevance, and optional contact details supplied by the person making the suggestion.

Registered accounts use authentication information to provide access. Draft case records and editorial tools are restricted to users with an administrator role.
